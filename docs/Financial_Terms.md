# Financil Market Terms

📈 Core Concepts of the Stock Market

🏛️ What Is the Stock Market?

- A decentralized network where investors buy and sell shares—units of ownership in publicly traded companies.

- Major exchanges include NYSE, Nasdaq, and LSE.
💼 Stocks vs. Shares
- Stock: General ownership in one or more companies.
- Share: Specific unit of ownership in a single company.
🧮 Market Participants
- Retail Investors: Individuals like you and me.
- Institutional Investors: Banks, hedge funds, pension funds.
- Market Makers: Entities that provide liquidity by continuously buying and selling.

💡 Essential Terms to Know

Term and Definitions

    - Bid/Ask Bid = price buyers offer; Ask = price sellers want. Spread = market liquidity.
    - Bull Market = Period of rising prices and investor optimism.
    - Bear Market =Period of falling prices and pessimism.
    - IPO =Initial Public Offering—when a company first sells shares to the public.
    - Dividend = Profit share paid to shareholders, often quarterly.
    - P/E Ratio = Price-to-Earnings ratio—used to value a stock relative to its earnings.
    - Volume = Number of shares traded in a given period.
    - Volatility = Degree of price fluctuation—higher means riskier.
    - Short Selling = Betting a stock will fall by borrowing and selling it, then buying it back cheaper.

🧠 Strategic Layers

📊 Fundamental Analysis

- Focuses on company financials: revenue, profit, debt, growth.
- Tools: P/E ratio, earnings reports, balance sheets.

📉 Technical Analysis

- Uses price charts and indicators to predict future movements.
- Tools: Moving averages, RSI, MACD, candlestick patterns.

🧠 Behavioral Finance

- Studies how psychology affects investor decisions (e.g., fear, greed, herd behavior).
