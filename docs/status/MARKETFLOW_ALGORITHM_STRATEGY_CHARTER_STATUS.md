## Algorithm Identity and Philosophy

MarketFlow is an expectancy-first trend-and-flow engine designed to identify tradable directional structure using price, volume, relative strength, volatility, regime context, and abstention/no-trade logic.

The charter prioritizes tradable expectancy, risk-adjusted opportunity, and abstention quality rather than classification accuracy alone. Its primary question is whether conditions have positive expected value after risk, costs, drawdown, and position-management constraints; directional or regime classification against simple baselines is secondary.
