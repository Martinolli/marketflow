## Risk Controls

- No execution without separate operator approval.
- No source-output or frozen-dataset mutation and no repair of META's `913` records.
- No provider access, acquisition, rerun, recomputation, model replay, strategy scoring, or recommendation.
- No predictive-usefulness or profitability acceptance and no runtime, paper, broker, or stitching authority.
- No raw provider payload or API-key storage/printing; all outputs remain research-only.

- Refinement execution, label/feature regeneration, validation reruns, metric recomputation, or model-comparison reruns.
- Predictive-usefulness or profitability acceptance.
- Runtime migration, strategy use, paper trading, broker execution, or trade recommendations.

- Fail closed on changed evidence, missing planning sections, unauthorized flags, or altered deterministic digests.
